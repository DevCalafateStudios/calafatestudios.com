Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 12, 2026

Calafate Studios ("we", "us") is an independent game studio based in Cyprus. This policy describes what happens to your data when you visit calafatestudios.com, contact us by email, or play our games. The short version: we collect as little as possible.

Our website

This website is a static page. It does not use accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) that are required to serve the site and protect it from abuse.

Email

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply and to fix the problem you reported. We do not sell it, share it, or add it to mailing lists.

Our games

Our games are designed to work without accounts and to collect the minimum data needed to run. Where a specific game uses third-party services (for example, advertising or analytics), it's disclosed below and in that game's App Store privacy details.

Kosynka

Kosynka does not require an account and does not collect personal information directly. The only data collection in the app comes from Google AdMob, which we use to show advertising (banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads). AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier) to serve and measure ads, and — if you consent — to personalize them. On first launch you'll see a consent form letting you choose whether ads are personalized, and on iOS you'll also see Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt; tracking-related identifiers are only used if you allow it. Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's own privacy policy. Kosynka does not use any other analytics or crash-reporting SDK, and your game progress and statistics stay on your device.

Children

Our games are made for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

If you are in the European Union, you have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you (in practice, this usually means your emails to us). Write to hello@calafatestudios.com and we will take care of it.

Changes

When our practices change — for example, when a game ships with new services — we will update this page and the effective date above.
